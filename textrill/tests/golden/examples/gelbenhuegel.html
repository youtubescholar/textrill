title: Drachen
description: The dragons of the East Lands tags:
---
The dragons of Gelbenhügel are genderless, solitary, and reproduce asexually. Individual dragons may adopt a female or male persona when dealing with creatures that possess the concept of gender.

Once a dragon reaches 1,000 years of age, it gains the power to create offspring. It takes the most magnificent gems, precious metals, and magical items it has in its horde, and uses its fiery breath to melt them down into a large, scintillating egg. Upon the egg it inscribes the draconic ideogram for "life."

It takes the egg and hides it far away from its lair, somewhere it believes it will never be discovered. Then it forgets about it. Over the course of a century, the egg absorbs the wesen around it, feeding the dragon embryo locked inside. Once it reaches maturity, the dragon breaks out of its egg, saving the fragments of the shell to begin its own horde.

Rarely, a dragon is unable to hatch from its egg. In these unlucky cases, the trapped dragon petrifies and becomes a steinherz, a "stone heart." The egg continues to absorb wesen, and over the centuries transforms into a wellspring of unimaginable magical energy.

title: About your world
description: Basic knowledge about the city of Gelbenhügel tags:
---
As a native of the glorious city of Gelbenhügel, there are a few things that your character would consider basic knowledge.

## Spirits are everywhere

Gelbenhügel and the lands that surround it are filled with spirits. They inhabit and animate mountains, lakes, rivers, trees, clouds, fields, and waterfalls. They cause the rain to fall, the rivers to flow, the wind to gust, and the crops to grow. They are the forces of nature, they are everywhere, and keeping them happy is the key to ensuring good fortune.

Because the spirits that animate the world are immanent in the daily lives of Gelbenhügeler, there isn't much debate about their shape, substance, or ethos. In fact, the relationship between an average person and the spirits they interact with is primarily transactional and hyper-local.

Farmers will take care of the spirits that inhabit their fields so that the crops will grow and the harvest will be bountiful. Fishermen tend to the spirits of the streams and river. Everyone pays homage to the ancestor spirits that watch over their families; the spirits are part of the fabric of daily life.

## Magic is everywhere

Gelbenhügel is filled with magical spells, powers, items, and effects. In these lands, magic is the result of manipulating Wesen, the vital essence that permeates all things. Some creatures entreat spirits to manipulate Wesen on their behalf while others are able to manipulate it directly. Some creatures produce magical effects by learning to control the Wesen within themselves and others produce them by using magical weapons, armor, and other wondrous items.

Everyone in Gelbenhügel expects to see and use magic in their everyday lives. Most peasants have one or two common magical items in their possession, alchemists offer basic medical care, and hedge mages use low-level magic to provide useful services.

## Portals are everywhere

The Great War destroyed lives, cultures, and entire empires but it also left the veils between worlds in tatters. Since the war ended, the Volk of Gelbenhügel have discovered a multitude of gates and portals that connect their city to other planes, worlds, and dimensions. Some of these portals are easily discernible, while others blend into the background of the city. Some take no effort to open, while others require considerable effort.

In addition, Gelbenhügel is filled with pocket dimensions -- worlds within worlds. The bottom of a forgotten well may be ten times the size it should be and contain an ancient slumbering horror, shrines to various spirits contain entire worlds, and mysterious staircases lead into impossible landscapes.

## Undead are everywhere

When a sentient being dies, its spirit typically moves from the physical world to the spirit world. The spirit's movement opens a conduit through which necrotic energy flows. Without a Geistliche to perform the proper rites and guide the spirit to the netherworld, the necrotic energy will reanimate the deceased creature's corpse, creating a zombie.

Spirits of Volk that die with unfinished business often remain trapped in the physical world, haunting a person or place until they can be put to rest. Others become trapped by the intensity of their emotions -- typically jealousy, rage, or hatred -- and spend eternity tormenting the living.

title: Adventuring
description: A guide to the adventuring life tags:

  • adventuring-company --- Adventurers are treasure hunters, explorers, troubleshooters, and, very often, first responders. Neophyte adventurers clear giant rats out of basements and locate lost heirlooms, while veteran adventurers foil the plots of mad wizards, destroy portals to the netherworlds, and fight off the rampaging forces of nature.

In addition to being popular among the citizenry because of their utility, adventurers are popular among the city's leadership because the guilds generate significant tax revenue. Inside city limits, adventurers have quasi-legal authority, so they are often given significant deference while in the midst of a quest.

While an average citizen who wants to talk to the mayor might be told to go talk to the city clerk to schedule an appointment that will be repeatedly delayed and rescheduled, an adventurer who wants to alert the authorities to a threat against the city will be ushered directly into the mayor's office.

## Laws of adventuring

Gelbenhügel's city council issues charters to adventuring companies and issues the laws by which they must abide.

  • **First**, all adventurers must belong to a chartered adventuring company to operate within the city and surrounding territory.
  • **Second**, violence between members of rival companies is prohibited and is punished by expulsion.
  • **Third**, members of an adventuring company may lay claim to a newly discovered passage or dungeon by placing their company's mark at the entrance of the dungeon. Once placed, other adventurers are expected to respect the claim, even when the claiming adventurer is no longer physically present.
  • **Fourth**, an adventurer may pass through and take treasure from another company's claim, but they are expected to remit half of any recovered treasure to the claiming company.
  • **Fifth**, claim disputes can be resolved by any judge or licensed arbitrator.
  • **Finally**, if an adventurer saves another adventurer from certain death, they are entitled to half of whatever treasure the other adventurer is carrying.

Gelbenhügeler mark the passage of time by counting the rotation of the sun disk and the orbits of the outer worlds. Each rotation of the sun disk, which takes approximately twenty hours, is recorded as one day. The outer worlds make one orbit around the world disk every 320 days, which is recorded as a year. Within the year, they divide the days into 40 groups of eight, called weeks.

Before the Great War, the Volk standardized the names of the days of the week for ease of commerce. In any given language, the days are known as:

  • First Day
  • Second Day
  • Third Day
  • Midweek
  • Fifth Day
  • Sixth Day
  • Seventh Day
  • Week End

Gelbenhügeler typically view Midweek as a day of rest and relaxation and Week End as a day for celebration and frivolity. Whenever there is not another festival being held on Week End, the clergy of the Spirit of the City hold Wochenendfestival, a massive, lager-fueled feast in the Volkshalle, beneath the colossal bronze statue of the city's founder.

The 320 day year has 4 major seasons and 20 micro-seasons, each two weeks long.

  • **Starkbierfest** -- the "strong beer festival" runs for three weeks in early spring. Held in each brewery instead of a central location, as each brewery produces its own version of a hearty, higher-proof beer.

title: Geography
description: The world on which Gelbenhügel exists tags:
---
Gelbenhügel exists on the eastern edge of Gilea, a flat, disk world orbited by an ever-increasing number of flat, disk moons. In the center of the disk is a supercontinent, referred to simply as the Continent, where the majority of Volk live. There, dwarves and dragons battle for supremacy in the mountains, elves and hags wrestle for control in the great forests, and humans fight off orcs on the coastal plains.

Surrounding the Continent are a number of island chains that stretch into the Ocean at the End of the World. At the edge of the disk, the ocean falls away into the Aether, creating a roiling ring of mist. The water vapor rises into the atmosphere where it cools, condenses, and creates precipitation.

In the center of the Continent are the Risafjöll, mountains so tall that they stretch beyond the atmosphere of Gilea directly into the Aether. Below the disk hang the Undirheima, massive stalactites that house an entire world of darkness-dwelling creatures.

The sun disk, Zon, orbits the perimeter of Gilea, projecting its warm, life-giving glow throughout the world over the course of roughly twenty hours. Zon's light cone is oriented in such a way that roughly half the surface of the disk is illuminated at any time, creating a natural day/night cycle. The Risafjöll are in perpetual twilight, as they are constantly at the edge of the light cone.

The climate is warmest near the edge of the disk, where the ocean meets the sun disk. The island chains are tropical, the farmlands near the coast are subtropical, the forests temperate, and the center of the continent is arctic.

At the heart of the Risafjöll is Íshjarta, a massive living glacier that is constantly growing and which replaces any water lost to the Aether at the disk's edge.

Greetings, adventurer!

On behalf of the leadership and staff of the [Perilous Peregrine Questing Company](../factions/perilous-peregrine-questing-company.md), allow me to extend a hearty welcome and congratulate you on your decision to affiliate yourself with the finest adventuring company in Gelbenhügel.

Within this guide you'll find useful information on the various goods and services our caring and attentive staff and shopkeepers provide. You'll also find helpful tips and tricks that you may find useful when navigating the dangerous and often deceptive lands that lay beyond our walls and below our feet.

If you are a student of ancient history, then you will recall that, before the Great War, the mighty empires of dwarves, elves, gnomes, and humans built a world of peace and prosperity through diplomacy and trade. Our great city is an artifact of that era -- at its height, we housed 350,000 Volk within our borders.

Then the [Heretics of Nichtswesens](../factions/heretics-of-nichtswesens.md) opened the Demon Gate and shattered the peace. Pestilence, famine, and war decimated our world, destroyed the mighty empires, and devastated our city. We were left with nothing but dilapidated ruins, hungry monsters, and murderous bandits...which, to the resilient Volk of Gelbenhügel, meant endless opportunity for power and glory!

Adventuring companies like ours powered the city's recovery by reclaiming treasures of ages past, protecting our citizens from fearsome monsters, and driving gangs of bandits and raiders far beyond our borders. You have chosen to follow in the storied footsteps of our city's bravest and boldest heroes.

So spread your wings, adventurer! Explore crumbling catacombs, slay ravenous beasts, and bring back as much of that glittering treasure of ages past as you can fit in your backpack.

May the spirits be ever in your favor,

[Andreas Stieler](../people/andreas-stieler.md), Gildenmeister

description: A magical beer stein
tags:

  • magic
  • rechargable --- No self-respecting adventurer would venture outside a city, let alone into a dark and dangerous dungeon without their maßkrug in hand. When this stoneware beer stein is filled with exactly one liter of liquid, you can use transform that liquid, no matter how putrid, fetid, or toxic, into exactly one liter of pure, thirst-quenching beer.

After one use, you can recharge the maßkrug by filling it with fresh beer, which is happily absorbed by the stein.

title: Zaubersenf
description: Magical mustard that makes mystery meat edible tags:

  • magic
  • consumable --- This magical yellow mustard makes edible any meat that it is applied to. You may apply one serving of zaubersenf to a cut of meat no larger than 16oz. The mustard immediately neutralizes any poisons or toxins present in the meat and renders it both tasty and delicious. Each jar contains ten servings.

"Karl! You've got to try this new wurst I just made!"

Karl stroked his beard and squinted at his diminutive adventuring companion. Jürgen hadn't brought any meat with them into the dungeon, so where in the blazes had he found the ingredients to make a piping hot wurst? The rats?

He looked at the full jar of zaubersenf sitting by the fire, looked back at his eager friend, and sighed -- better not to ask.

title: Questing Medallion
description: A magical medallion that allows for a quick and safe return tags:

  • magic
  • rechargable --- Powered by the patron spirits of the guild, the Questing Medallion is the primary benefit of membership.

As an action, a member in good standing may speak the command word and be teleported back to their guild house. This command will work anywhere within the borders of the world of Gilea, but does not allow the individual to be transported across planar boundaries.

Recharge the medallion by donating 100 gold to the guild's operating funds.
